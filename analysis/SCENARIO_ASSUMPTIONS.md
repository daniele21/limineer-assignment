# Conservative and potential scenarios

The assumptions are in `assumptions.md`. Both scenarios use the same sponsor rubric. The conservative input remains unchanged.

| Decision | Conservative | Potential / risky |
|---|---|---|
| Grid evidence | Use official figures. Hold missing data. | Consider a newer vendor estimate if official data is missing or predates June 30, 2026. Use its upper band for upside. |
| Unknown sentiment | Earns no points. | Earns ten points if support is confirmed and any stated condition is met. |
| Unknown distance | Hold the site. | Earns 15 points if a new check confirms distance ≤1 km. |
| Other unknowns | Earn no points; hold critical gaps. | Show favourable outcomes as explicit hypotheses. |
| Protection/area conflicts | Hold until checked. | Show conditional potential, but require a legal footprint and verified usable area before recommendation. |

Known facts stay in both scenarios. Refusal and confirmed protection remain exclusions. Opposition, flood risk and sold land do not disappear. Duplicate parcels remain one site.

Use only these two score endpoints. Keep each contribution and its supporting evidence or hypothesis visible. The vendor central estimate is a diagnostic, not a third scenario score or an expected value.

Held sites have no conservative ranked score. For example, S-024 could reach 100, but 55 points depend on favourable unknown outcomes, and protection must also be cleared. Keep such cases separate from today's candidates.

S-013 stays held. Its reported roughly 700 m² earns zero area points in the potential case; its sold land is not restored. That approximate area has no measured error range.

These are scores under assumptions, not guaranteed bounds or confidence intervals. The vendor band is unvalidated, and parcel area is still a proxy for usable land.

The comparison in `output/scenario_review.json` covers all 39 physical opportunities: 23 current candidates, 14 conditional cases and two exclusions. It retains criterion contributions, hypotheses and input hashes. The raw data and conservative scores are unchanged.
