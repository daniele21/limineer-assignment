    Inspect the scoring method ↗

    Each criterion earns a tier from 0 to 5. Its contribution is weight × tier ÷ 5. Contributions add up to a maximum of 100.

    Conservative: official figures and recorded evidence. Unknown support earns no extra points and remains unknown. Sites with critical gaps stay on hold.

    Risky upside: favourable, unverified assumptions, including eligible vendor estimates and support if confirmed. This is a scenario score, not a probability or guaranteed upper bound.

    Parcel area is a proxy for usable land. A signed letter of intent is non-binding. Protection and owner refusal always exclude a site. Duplicate parcels are counted once.
